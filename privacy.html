Privacy Policy

Nailed by Mari · Cleveland, OH · Last updated July 23, 2026

This policy describes how Nailed by Mari ("we," "us") collects and uses your information when you visit this website, chat with us, or book an appointment.

Information we collect

When you book an appointment or contact us, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, appointment preferences, design notes, and any photos you share with us. We do not collect payment card information on this website.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide our services: confirming and managing your appointments, sending appointment-related text messages and emails, responding to your questions, and keeping a record of your visits so we can serve you better.

SMS / text messaging

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from Nailed by Mari related to your inquiry and appointments (confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that operate our booking, calendar, and messaging systems, and only as needed to run our business. We do not buy, sell, or trade leads.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To have your information corrected or deleted, contact us on Instagram at @nailedbymariii.
